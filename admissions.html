Admissions & Enrollment

Join a community dedicated to academic rigor, discipline, and personal growth. Follow our straightforward 3-step admission pathway.

A WELCOMING ENVIRONMENT

Beginning Your Journey at Gateway

We understand that choosing the right school for your child is one of the most critical decisions your family will make.

At Gateway School Complex, our admissions process is designed to be supportive, transparent, and learner-focused. We welcome learners transferring from other schools as well as children embarking on their foundational elementary years.

Our goal during enrollment is to understand each child's academic background, strengths, and areas where they need mentorship, ensuring a smooth transition into our classroom community.

Our 3-Step Admissions Process

A simple, orderly procedure to guide you from initial inquiry to your child's first day of school.

01

Make an Enquiry

DOCUMENTATION

Application Requirements Checklist

Please prepare the following documentation when submitting your child's admission packet. Standard Guidelines

Standard Registration Documents

Required for all new applicants across Elementary and JHS.

Completed and signed official Gateway Application Form
Certified copy of child's official Birth Certificate
Two (2) recent passport-sized photographs of the applicant
Copy of child's immunization & medical certification report

Academic Records & Transfer Details

Required for learners entering Primary 2 through JHS 3.

Official terminal report cards from the immediate previous academic year
Transfer certificate or clearance letter from the previous school (if applicable)
Participation in brief diagnostic placement check in Literacy & Numeracy
Parent/Guardian National ID copy (for administrative contact records)
GRADE PLACEMENT

Age & Grade Criteria

Placement is based on chronological age guidelines coupled with prior learning background and diagnostic review.

Educational Division Grade Levels Typical Age Range Placement Assessment Focus
Lower Elementary Primary 1 – Primary 3 6 – 8 Years Foundational phonics, basic reading, writing motor skills, number recognition
Upper Elementary Primary 4 – Primary 6 9 – 11 Years Reading comprehension, arithmetic operations, basic science awareness
Junior High School (JHS 1) JHS 1 12 – 13 Years Elementary completion mastery, basic algebra & geometry, English composition
Junior High School (JHS 2 – 3) JHS 2 – JHS 3 13 – 15 Years Detailed transcript review, subject readiness, High School transition preparation
