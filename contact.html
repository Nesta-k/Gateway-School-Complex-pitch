Visiting Policy

All visitors are required to register at the main security gate upon arrival. A security visitor tag will be issued for access to the administration block.

Tour Appointments

While walk-in enquiries are welcomed, scheduling a visit in advance ensures that an administrative officer is reserved to guide you through classrooms.

Office Hours

Monday – Friday: 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Saturday: By prior appointment for working parents. The school office is closed on Sundays and statutory holidays.
