PRIMARY EDUCATION

Elementary School Program

Guiding young learners through their most vital formative years by cultivating curiosity, strong phonics and reading habits, numeracy problem-solving, and positive social behavior.

Division Scope Lower & Upper Primary
FOUNDATIONAL LEARNING

Nurturing Curiosity & Confident Literacy

Our primary classrooms foster active participation, sensory discovery, and phonics mastery, ensuring every child develops a genuine love for reading and structured enquiry.

Core Learning Areas

Our elementary curriculum is designed to stimulate intellectual curiosity while establishing solid mastery of fundamental concepts.

Literacy & Phonics

Reading comprehension, vocabulary building, creative writing, and guided oral articulation.

Foundational Mathematics

Number operations, mental arithmetic, geometric concepts, and applied word problems.

Natural Science Discovery

Observation of living systems, environmental care, simple physical forces, and health habits.

ICT & Computer Basics

Safe computer operation, digital keyboard skills, educational games, and basic software literacy.

Creative & Expressive Arts

Drawing, painting, craft construction, cultural music, and drama recitals that foster imaginative confidence.

Our World & Our People

Social ethics, community roles, national symbols, geography, and mutual respect among diverse traditions.

Small, focused classroom sizes ensuring individual attention.

UPPER SECONDARY PREPARATION

Junior High School (JHS 1 – 3)

Equipping adolescent learners with independent study habits, deep subject comprehension, laboratory experimentation, and disciplined preparation for senior secondary education.

Division Scope JHS 1, JHS 2 & JHS 3
ADVANCED PREPARATION

Subject Depth, Technology & Analytical Rigor

Adolescent learners engage in hands-on science practicals, ICT computing research, and structured group debate, instilling discipline and academic excellence for Senior High School.

Academic Discipline & Curriculum Subjects

At the JHS level, students engage in comprehensive subject depth led by specialist teachers, combining theory with practical application.

English Language & Literature

Grammar mastery, essay composition, prose, poetry appreciation, and advanced comprehension.

Core Mathematics

Algebra, plane and solid geometry, statistics, probability, ratios, and real-world quantitative analysis.

Integrated Science

Foundations of biology, chemistry, physics, agricultural science, and laboratory inquiry.

Social Studies

Governance, civic rights, environmental sustainability, African history, and socioeconomic growth.

Computing / ICT

Digital research, office productivity tools, basic logic, online safety, and emerging technology awareness.

Career Technology & Pre-Technical

Technical drawing, basic materials science, design thinking, and practical hands-on problem solving.

Religious & Moral Education (RME)

Ethical reasoning, personal integrity, family values, and community harmony.

Ghanaian Language & French

Cultural communication, bilingual vocabulary, conversational fluency, and grammatical structure.

Diagnostic mock evaluations & continuous transition guidance for High School.

OUR PEDAGOGY

Teaching Methodology & Assessment

An intentional blend of structured instruction, collaborative group work, and regular developmental monitoring.

Concept Comprehension Over Rote

We emphasize understanding the "why" behind mathematical formulas and scientific principles so learners apply knowledge rather than simply memorize text.

Continuous Diagnostic Assessment

Regular class exercises, weekly check-ins, and project assignments identify learning gaps early, allowing educators to offer targeted reinforcement.

Transparent Parent Reporting

Comprehensive end-of-term academic reports and dedicated parent-teacher conferences keep families actively involved in their child's developmental path.

CO-CURRICULAR LIFE

Clubs & Student Development

Beyond textbooks, learners discover personal passions, practice team collaboration, and develop poise and leadership skills.

Science & Innovation

STEM & Discovery Club

Hands-on experiments, introductory coding logic, recycling projects, and scientific exhibitions that nurture inquisitive young problem solvers.

Oratory & Writing

Debate & Literary Society

Cultivating poise, logical argumentation, public speaking confidence, and literature reviews across Elementary and JHS divisions.

Creative Arts

Cultural Troupe & Music

Choral singing, traditional percussion, poetry recitations, and theatrical dramatizations honoring Ghanaian cultural heritage and artistic expression.

Athletics & Health

Sports & Athletics Teams

Football, track and field, volleyball, table tennis, and teamwork drills building physical stamina, endurance, and sporting camaraderie.

Civic Responsibility

Cadet Corps & Leadership

Precision marching drills, time management, peer mentorship, and civic duties teaching self-control, dignity, and civic leadership.

Environmental Care

Green Campus & Eco Club

School gardening, tree planting, campus cleanliness stewardship, and environmental sustainability awareness for healthy communities.
